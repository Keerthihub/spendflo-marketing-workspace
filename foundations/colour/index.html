Colour

A warm-stone neutral core, a signature deep maroon for dark surfaces, and a single magenta accent from the Flo mark. Product UI stays near-monochrome; brand surfaces bring the maroon and magenta forward.

Signature

The deep maroon is the heart of the brand’s dark surfaces — covers, dividers, the sidebar you’re looking at. Magenta is the accent and CTA colour, drawn from the Flo mark.

Warm neutrals

Every neutral leans warm — stone, not slate. Surfaces step quietly from page to inner-card, then to the near-black used for product footers.

Text & borders

Data & status

Content that shows numbers — KPI cards, charts, status pills — draws from a small data palette. These never appear as brand or UI colour; they belong to data.
