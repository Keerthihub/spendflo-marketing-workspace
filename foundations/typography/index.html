Two families

PP Neue Montreal (Medium, 500) carries H1 and H2 and nothing else. Inter handles H3, body, nav, buttons, and captions. A single monospace accent appears once per surface at most — the format tags in the sidebar are exactly that.

Rules

  • Letter-spacing is 0 everywhere; the only exception is −0.01em on H1.
  • Display type is H1/H2 only. Never set body, nav, or buttons in PP Neue Montreal.
  • Numbers in decks and cards can use display weight for impact — always with a label beneath.
