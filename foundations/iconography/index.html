Rules

  • Stroke only — no filled icons, no duotone.
  • Icons inherit currentColor and follow the text tint beside them.
  • Icon-only controls always carry an aria-label.
  • The magenta accent on icons is reserved for the brand DLS chrome — inside product and content, icons stay monochrome.
