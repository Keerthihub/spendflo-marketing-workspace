Anatomy

  • Maroon header — customer name, a “Case study” pill, and the headline result in display type.
  • Metric row — three outcomes, each a figure and a label.
  • Three columns — challenge, what Flo did, outcome. Tight, skimmable, no filler.
  • One page. If it needs two, it’s a deck, not a case study.
